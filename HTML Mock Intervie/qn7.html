About

Kauvery Hospital is a leading multi-specialty healthcare chain in India, founded in 1999 in Tiruchirappalli (Trichy) by a team of doctors including Dr. Chandrakumar and Dr. Manivannan. It started as a 30-bed facility and has expanded into a network with over 2,500 beds across multiple cities.
