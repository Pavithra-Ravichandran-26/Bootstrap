Vannila Cake Recipe

  1. Beat egg and Vannila esscence
  2. Add powder sugar and beat till sugar mixed with egg
  3. Now add all dry ingrediated and mix by cut and fold method
  4. Pour the mixture to Tin and Bake for 25 to 30 min at 180deg
